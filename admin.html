🎖️ Honour Wall — Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?
  1. Har section mein Week label likho (e.g. "Week 1st — May 2025")
  2. Har rank ke liye Name aur Stat value bharo
  3. Circle pe click karke DP Upload karo — Cloudinary pe jayegi
  4. "🖼️ Upload Frame" se avatar frame upload karo (optional overlay)
  5. Upar "💾 Save & Export" dabao — rizz-data.json download hogi ✅
